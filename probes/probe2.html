No. 42 Trolley and Trailer, produced by Carlisle & Finch

Carlisle & Finch is a manufacturer of nautical equipment in Cincinnati, Ohio. For most of its history, the company has focused on searchlights, especially for marine applications; variants of this design have also been used for navigation beacons by airports and lighthouses. The company is considered the first in the US to produce electric toy trains, introducing innovations such as using the rails to conduct electrical power to the cars.
